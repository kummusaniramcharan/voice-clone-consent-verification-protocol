Anti-Scam Checklist & Response Guide

Criminals only need 3 to 10 seconds of someone’s voice scraped from Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube to clone their voice convincingly. Use these verification tactics before sending money or giving up information.

The 6 Core Rules of Vocal Verification

Follow these steps immediately if you receive a distress, arrest, or urgent financial request over the phone.

1

Hang Up Immediately and Call Back on a Known Number

Scammers manipulate caller ID displays (Caller ID spoofing) so their incoming call appears to originate from a bank, police station, or relative. Disconnect the call, do not press any redial button, and manually dial your saved contact number or the bank’s official phone number printed on the back of your debit card.

2

Establish a Private Family Safe-Word

Agree on a private, memorable codeword or passphrase with your children, parents, and partner. The safe-word must never be shared online, messaged over social media, or written where others can view it. In an emergency, ask for the safe-word immediately; an AI cloner cannot guess it.

3

Beware of Manufactured Urgency & Demands for Secrecy

AI voice scams depend on inducing panic ("I’ve had a car accident", "I'm in police custody", "Wire funds within 15 minutes or face arrest"). Scammers insist that you stay on the phone and forbid you from telling other family members. High emotional pressure is the #1 signature of fraud.

4

Never Share OTPs, Passwords, or Wire Funds to Safe Accounts

No legitimate bank officer, law enforcement agent, or company executive will ever demand one-time SMS verification passwords (OTPs), PINs, or request money transfers to "secure holding accounts" or cryptocurrency wallets.

5

Ask a Specific Question Only the Real Person Could Answer

Ask an intimate, offline question that is impossible to scrape from public social media profiles. Examples: "What color was our first dog’s collar?", "Which teacher did you dislike in 6th grade?", or "Where did we go for dinner two Tuesdays ago?".

6

Understand the Anatomy of "Digital Arrest" Fraud

Scammers posing as CBI, Customs, or Mumbai Cyber Crime claim your Aadhaar/Passport is linked to illegal narcotics or money laundering. They demand you stay on a Skype/WhatsApp video call ("digital arrest") and transfer money to "RBI verification accounts". Fact: No legitimate police force or judicial court in India or abroad conducts arrests, bail hearings, or money transfers over video calls. Hang up immediately.

7

Activate VoiceClone Live Protection During Suspicious Calls

Put the call on speakerphone and launch VoiceClone's Live Tracker. The on-device engine continuously calculates AI voice probability and flags extortion/banking keywords in real time, triggering full-screen warning shields and evidence logs.

🚨 If You Have Already Sent Money or Disclosed Financial Details

Time is the decisive factor in freezing fraudulent transactions:

  1. Call your bank fraud department within minutes: State clearly: "I am a victim of an active authorized push payment fraud scam. Please initiate a fraudulent payment recall immediately." (In India, dial 1930 immediately to freeze account rails).
  2. Freeze your credit reports: If sensitive personal info or social security/Aadhaar/national IDs were revealed, place a fraud alert or credit freeze with national credit bureaus.
  3. Preserve evidence: Take screenshots of phone call logs, note exact timestamps, record the voice sample if possible, and save transaction reference IDs.
