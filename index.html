How Live Scam Detection Works (Honest Privacy Guarantee)

Web browsers are restricted by operating system sandboxes and cannot silently intercept cellular phone calls. VoiceClone connects to real audio using 3 supported methods:

  • 1. Microphone: Put incoming phone call on speakerphone beside your device
  • 2. Tab / System Audio: Screen/tab sharing audio for WhatsApp Web, Google Meet, Zoom, Discord
  • 3. Demo Audio Stream: Test the detector with bundled human vs synthetic AI voice recordings
