System Architecture & Acoustics

How VoiceClone Scans Speech in Your Browser

VoiceClone uses standard Web Audio API DSP to extract 24 physical vocal cues, standardizes them against a trained L2-regularized logistic model, and computes a calibrated authenticity verdict—100% inside your browser memory.

The 4-Step Analysis Pipeline

Every byte of audio is decoded, measured, and classified locally on your device in real time.

1

Audio Decode & Quality Gate

Audio is decoded with AudioContext.decodeAudioData(), mixed to mono, and resampled to 16,000 Hz using OfflineAudioContext. Quality checks inspect duration (min 3s), clipping percentage (max 5%), silence proportion (max 70%), and RMS loudness (min -45 dBFS).

2

DSP Feature Extraction

A pure DSP module computes 24 exact cues across 4 domains: recording noise floor & bandwidth, Hann-windowed FFT spectral flatness & 26-filter MFCC timbre variance, autocorrelation pitch (F0 jitter, flat plateaus, HNR), and pause cadence dynamics.

3

Trained Neural & DSP Model

Audio is processed with fine-tuned wav2vec2 ONNX models (running via WebGPU with WASM fallback) alongside regularized logistic regression on acoustic features, delivering sub-150ms live window inference without cloud dependency.

4

Calibrated Verdict & Explanations

The logit produces a sigmoid probability P(AI), displayed as an AI likelihood percentage. The top 3 contributing acoustic features are highlighted in plain English with comparative reference values from the training dataset.

Real-Time Protection Engine

How the Live Scam-Call Voice Tracker Operates

Unlike batch file analyzers, the Live Tracker operates as an active call shield. It evaluates live audio continuously, maintains sliding state memory, and monitors spoken dialogue patterns simultaneously.

1. 16 kHz AudioWorklet Streaming

Continuous 16 kHz Float32 PCM is captured from Microphone (speakerphone mode) or Tab audio via getDisplayMedia. Audio is buffered in a 48,000-sample ring buffer, slicing 3.0-second sliding windows every 1.0 second (2.0s overlap).

2. Voice Activity Detection (VAD)

Energy thresholds and Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) filter out background silence and room echo. Silent frames are bypassed to prevent false neural triggers and reduce battery consumption.

3. EMA Smoothing & Hysteresis

Scores from the last 30 windows are integrated via Exponential Moving Average (α = 0.30). A 3-window state debounce ensures random acoustic spikes never trigger false alarms.

4. Multi-Modal Cross Escalation

Combines AI Voice probability (50%), scam keyword density (35%), and urgency pressure (15%). When synthetic voice correlates with critical extortion or "digital arrest" terms, synergy multipliers escalate the threat index above 88/100.

Honest Mobile Limitations & Architecture

Standard web browsers cannot access cellular call downlinks due to Android 10+ and iOS security sandbox restrictions. VoiceClone recommends putting phone calls on speakerphone beside your laptop or phone microphone for acoustic capture, or using the tab audio source for WhatsApp/Meet calls.

Physical Cues Analyzed by VoiceClone

Text-to-speech models and neural vocoders assemble speech from discrete phonetic representations. This introduces measurable acoustic anomalies across pitch regularity, pause dynamics, and harmonic clarity:

Natural Human Vocal Cues

  • Continuous Pitch Drift: Human vocal cords undergo involuntary micro-vibrations, yielding continuous sub-Hz frequency variations rather than rigid steps.
  • Dynamic Shimmer & Jitter: Amplitude and frequency fluctuate frame-to-frame during natural speech respiration.
  • Natural Breathing & Room Tone: Pauses between spoken phrases contain ambient room tone and inhalation sounds, rather than mathematical zero-amplitude gaps.
  • Dynamic Articulation: Formant frequencies shift gradually as the vocal tract, tongue, and lips change physical position.

Synthetic AI Audio Traces

  • Monotone Pitch Plateaus: Synthetic speech often locks onto exact fundamental frequencies across adjacent frames (<0.5 Hz difference).
  • Overly Pure Harmonics (High HNR): Neural synthesizers frequently render overtone tracks that lack natural vocal roughness or breathiness.
  • Digital Silence Gaps: Artificial synthesis engines often clamp pause energy to absolute digital zero (RMS < 1e-5).
  • Uniform Pause Rhythm: Synthetic phrasing often spaces sentences with mechanically consistent pause intervals (low pause CV).

In-Browser Custom Training Lab

Because vocal synthesis models evolve rapidly, VoiceClone provides an integrated Training Lab. You can import your own real and synthetic voice clips, apply synthetic data augmentations (gain, pink noise, low-pass filtering, room reverb, and bit quantization) to prevent hardware bias, and fit a custom L2-regularized logistic model.

Validation employs Grouped Cross-Validation by Source, guaranteeing that clips from any single microphone or AI engine never appear in both training and test sets. You can export the resulting model as a portable model.json or activate it immediately in your browser.
